Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: a SINGLE full-screen 16:9 art-directed game menu concept for Worms revival. A finished visual proposal, not production assets and not a web page.
Input image 1: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Match the expressive adult cartoon worms, painterly colour and strong ink silhouettes. Do not reproduce the reference composition.
Creative direction: "combat comic book". Imagine an irreverent early-2000s artillery game's match setup screen redesigned by an excellent comic-book graphic designer. The graphic language comes from the worms themselves: elastic silhouettes, bold ink contours, hand-lettered headings, controlled slanted cuts, small patches of print halftone. Clean intentional composition with breathing space, not a noisy collage.
Composition: a continuous warm ivory printed-comic page, with a large asymmetrical painted meadow battlefield panel occupying the right 55 percent of the screen. The left 40 percent is a character-led team setup, not a stack of generic cards. Logo at upper left, exact text "Worms revival" in lively restrained hand-lettering with dark ink outline. Below it a small black printed tab reading "НОВЫЙ МАТЧ".
Left side: two horizontally arranged teams, each with THREE large expressive worm busts peeking up from a single simple team-colour brush underline. First lineup has three distinct tough soldier worms, each with different face / army headwear. Second lineup has three distinct grumpy elderly worms, including one elderly woman. They feel like the actual selectable heroes, not tiny thumbnails or decorations. Under the first lineup, an ink heading "СПЕЦНАЗ" and a compact plain control line "Человек · Брутальные". Under the second, "ПЕНСИОНЕРЫ" and "Человек · Старые". A small plain "+ КОМАНДА" action near these, no box around it. Natural irregular ink rules, no ornamental hardware.
Right battlefield panel: actual isolated destructible grassy island terrain and turquoise water painted in the reference style, a miniature battlefield that reads as a useful playable map preview. Light blue sky. No huge battle explosion. Its thin ink frame has one slight angled corner and crosses the page gutter elegantly. Above it exact heading "ПОЛЕ БОЯ". Beneath the preview, compact horizontal typographic selectors on ivory, exact text "ЛУГ", "ОСТРОВА", "КЛАССИКА", each with a very small hand-inked downward chevron. A small "ДРУГАЯ КАРТА" action. No conventional HTML input fields.
Bottom area: secondary plain ink actions "ОСОБЫЕ ПРАВИЛА" and "ПОВТОР" toward left/center, and ONE bold primary action "В БОЙ!" near lower right. The primary action is a controlled burnt-orange brush swash with a crisp dark ink boundary and large cream hand lettering, not a metal button, explosion badge or glossy blob. The visual hierarchy must make choosing teams, inspecting map and starting the match immediately obvious.
Colours: warm ivory and almost-black ink as the graphic base; meadow blue/green in the map; limited coral-red and olive team accents; burnt orange only for primary action. Paper texture nearly invisible. Character illustration richly painted but restrained UI graphics.
Constraints: full-screen game interface, no device or physical desk, no browser chrome, no photographic realism, no generic dashboard, no glassmorphism, no dark teal translucent cards, no brass, no screws, no thick metal frames, no hazard stripes, no glowing gradients, no excessive grunge, no random stickers, no unrelated decorations. All text should be accurate Cyrillic as specified; no additional microtext. This should look like a deliberately designed illustrated game, with wit and personality, not an AI-generated fantasy control panel.
